## What this screen is for

Follow a structured 5-step onboarding path with automatic checkpoint saving at every step. You can pause anytime and resume from the exact step you left off, even after signing out.

## In 3 steps

1. Choose the journey matching your role (Tech Lead, Security Admin, or Operations Manager).
2. Fill in each step in order (Team, Workspace, Source, AI Model, Verification) and click "Validate and continue".
3. Optionally click "Provision demo workspace" to spin up a ready-to-explore sample environment.

## A real example

A new Tech Lead starts the "Tech Lead / Delivery" wizard, sets up her Team and first Workspace in steps 1 and 2, closes the browser, and resumes right at step 3 the next day.

## Common pitfalls

- **Step 5 reports incomplete verification**: verify that a valid source and AI model were saved in steps 3 and 4.
- **Need to edit a previous step**: click "Previous step" without losing your saved progress.
- **Journey labels do not fit your role**: switch profiles from the top selector on the card anytime.
