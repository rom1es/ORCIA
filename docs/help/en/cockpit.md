## What this screen is for

The Cockpit is your daily control center. It highlights only items that genuinely require human judgment — quick tie-breaks, strategic authoring anchors, and deliverable reviews — without cluttering your view with noise.

## In 3 steps

1. Check your active context (Team or Space) at the top of the left sidebar to filter visible decisions.
2. Follow the onboarding guide if setup steps remain open, or launch a process directly from the action bar.
3. Handle pending items: write your human framing anchor or resolve option tie-breaks with a short justification.

## A real example

After an automated compliance check, Nadia opens the Cockpit in the morning, sees a pending choice between two architecture options, writes a one-sentence justification, and confirms: the process immediately resumes to completion.

## Common pitfalls

- **The submit button on the violet card stays disabled**: verify that your personal authoring text has at least 30 characters and your signature is filled in.
- **No workspace available to start a process**: create a Workspace first in "My Spaces" and link it to a Team.
- **Too many decisions shown**: use the Team and Space selectors in the left sidebar to narrow down to your active scope.
