## What this screen is for

This screen mirrors your company's real org chart. Divisions group Teams; each Team owns its members, team leads, and AI billing attribution for its Workspaces.

## In 3 steps

1. Create a Division (BU) and assign one or more Division Leads (and optionally a Glossary Lead).
2. Add Teams under that Division and choose whether billing is active on each Team.
3. Click "Members" on a Team row to attach existing users or invite new colleagues.

## A real example

The administrator creates the "Finance & Risk" Division, assigns Nadia as Division Lead, and adds the "Payments Compliance" Team. Nadia can then manage her Team members and Workspaces independently without involving a global administrator.

## Common pitfalls

- **Cannot delete a Division or Team**: for safety, an organization unit can only be deleted once all child Teams and dependent access rules are removed.
- **A team member cannot run My Assistant**: confirm they are attached to a Team with active billing.
- **Confusing Teams and Workspaces**: Teams define people and budgets (here in My Organization); Workspaces define projects and documents (in My Spaces).
