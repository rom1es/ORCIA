## What this screen is for

This dashboard unifies financial control and operational mastery: internal costs by process and model tier, external service and remote agent spending, budget alerts, and human review quality metrics. It also links directly to the Activity Log.

## In 3 steps

1. Pick an observation scope (Global Platform, Division, Team, or Workspace) at the top of the dashboard.
2. Click "Configure budget cap" to set your monthly limit in euros, warning alert (e.g. 80%), and hard stop at 100%.
3. Switch across "Processes", "Model tiers", "External services", and "Human mastery" tabs to inspect spend or reset a paused connector.

## A real example

The Finance lead sets a €250 monthly budget on the Product team with an 80% warning. When deep analyses approach the cap, an alert fires so she can either grant a justified budget override or rebalance model tiers.

## Common pitfalls

- **All counters show €0 initially**: figures populate as real executions run in My Assistant and My Processes.
- **An external connector is paused**: open the "External services & agents" tab, inspect the pause reason, and reset the circuit once quotas are adjusted.
- **Need to audit who ran an action?** Click the "Activity Log" shortcut at the top of FinOps or open it from the Notifications bell.
