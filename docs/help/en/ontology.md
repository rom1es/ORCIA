## What this screen is for

Keep teams and AI agents speaking the exact same language. Any term defined in a Workspace can be proposed for promotion to Team, Division, or Global Platform scope after review by a designated lead.

## In 3 steps

1. Browse existing concepts filtered by level (Platform, Division, Team, or Workspace).
2. Add a new business term with a clear definition and optional tailoring per user profile.
3. Propose promoting a validated local term upward so the entire Division or organization benefits from it.

## A real example

The Payments team defines "T+2 Settlement Window" inside its Workspace. Recognizing its broader value, the Division's Glossary Lead approves promoting it to Division scope so every new project inherits it automatically.

## Common pitfalls

- **Cannot approve a Division-level promotion**: only a platform administrator or the Division's designated Glossary Lead can approve upward promotions.
- **A term shows a local variation**: the most specific scope (Workspace) refines the definition for that project while respecting platform-wide directives.
- **Where is the Glossary in the sidebar?** Open "My Spaces" and click the "Glossary" link at the top of the screen.
