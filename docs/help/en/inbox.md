## What this screen is for

This screen lets you see at a glance what needs human follow-up across your team: pending approvals, tasks currently claimed by a teammate, or items released for immediate pickup.

## In 3 steps

1. Check your active Space in the left sidebar to filter the displayed notifications.
2. Claim an available task so teammates immediately know you are handling it.
3. Release the task if you need to hand it off, or open the Cockpit directly to finalize the decision.

## A real example

When a compliance review arrives in the "Payments Product" Space, Nadia clicks "Claim" in the Inbox: her teammates instantly see the review is in progress, preventing duplicate work.

## Common pitfalls

- **No tasks appear in the list**: verify that a Space is selected or that a process has emitted a team notification.
- **A task appears locked by a colleague**: wait until their claim window ends or ask them to release it from their Inbox.
- **Looking for the full historical audit trail**: the Inbox focuses on active team coordination; open the Activity Log to inspect past operations.
