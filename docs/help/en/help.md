## What this screen is for

Opened from "Help" in the main navigation, this center gives you quick orientation across ORCIA and direct access to the living product showcase and verified capabilities catalog.

## In 3 steps

1. Read the overview and expand "How to proceed step by step", "Key concepts", and "Frequently asked questions".
2. Click "Open ORCIA Showcase" to explore delivered features, installed packs, and release history.
3. On any screen, click "Help" in the top-right bar or any `ⓘ` icon to view the guide tailored to that screen.

## A real example

A new teammate explores ORCIA for the first time: she opens "Help" to learn the core concepts, then clicks an `ⓘ` icon in My Spaces to see how to connect her team's documentation sources.

## Common pitfalls

- **Looking for help on a specific setting on another screen?** Click the `ⓘ` icon next to the heading to open and highlight that section in the right column.
- **A help article was edited by mistake**: an administrator can restore the previous version in one click.
- **Need a context-aware answer?** Click "Ask My Assistant about this screen" at the bottom of the help column.
