## What this screen is for

Opened from "Help" in the main navigation, this center gives you quick orientation across ORCIA and direct access to the living product showcase and verified capabilities catalog.

## In 3 steps

1. Read the overview and expand "How to proceed step by step", "Key concepts", and "Frequently asked questions".
2. Click "Documentation" in the page header to explore delivered features, installed packs, and release history.
3. On any screen, click "Help" in the top-right bar to open or close the guide tailored to that screen.

## A real example

A new teammate explores ORCIA for the first time: she opens "Help" to learn the core concepts, then opens the top-right "Help" panel inside My Spaces to see how to connect her team's documentation sources.

## Common pitfalls

- **Looking for help on another screen?** Click the "Help" button in the top-right bar to open the contextual help column for the active view.
- **A help article was edited by mistake**: an administrator can restore the previous version in one click.
- **Need a context-aware answer?** Click "Ask My Assistant about this screen" at the bottom of the help column.
