## What this screen is for

Browse all reusable assets across your organization in one place. Check each item's visibility scope (draft, Workspace, Team, or organization-wide) and promote proven assets for broader adoption.

## In 3 steps

1. Search or filter assets by category (process, agent, skill, or deliverable template).
2. Inspect an item to view its active version, permissions, and provenance.
3. Share or promote a proven resource to a wider scope so other teams do not start from scratch.

## A real example

After battle-testing a contract review template in the Procurement Space, Marc publishes it to the Division Catalog so every legal team can reuse it in one click.

## Common pitfalls

- **A resource is invisible to another team**: make sure its visibility scope has been promoted to Team, Division, or Organization level.
- **Looking specifically for AI agents?** Use "My Agents" in the main sidebar to configure agents and tool access in detail.
- **An older version is still active**: inspect the resource details to verify which version is currently published.
