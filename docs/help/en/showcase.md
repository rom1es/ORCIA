## What this screen is for

This living showcase stays synchronized with the capabilities actually delivered on your instance. It helps business teams, sponsors, and architects understand what ORCIA supports and how modules work together.

## In 3 steps

1. Switch across the 4 tabs: Macro vision, Features, Architecture, and Release changelog.
2. Inspect live summary counters (screens, components, installed packs, and verified capabilities).
3. Use the search box in the changelog to quickly locate a feature report or release note.

## A real example

Ahead of onboarding a new business unit, Nadia opens `/showcase` to walk stakeholders through workspace isolation, human-led governance, and euro cost tracking, then filters the changelog on recent improvements.

## Common pitfalls

- **How do I return to my workspace from the showcase?** Use the left sidebar or the top breadcrumb to jump back to Cockpit or Help.
- **A capability shows as planned**: only capabilities marked as delivered are active in the application menus.
- **Want an offline overview?** The showcase matches the static catalog generated in the project documentation.
