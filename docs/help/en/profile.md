## What this screen is for

Opened from "My Account" at the bottom of the left sidebar, your Profile summarizes who you are in ORCIA, which scopes you belong to, and which agents or processes you rely on most.

## In 3 steps

1. Review your identity, primary role, and Division/Team memberships (including billing status).
2. Check your personal activity metrics: token usage, daily peak, and active streak.
3. Click a popular shortcut or "Edit account settings" to update your password, language, or default business hat.

## A real example

Before starting work on a new Space, Claire opens her Profile to verify she belongs to the Payments Team with active billing, then jumps straight into her favorite workflow from her shortcuts.

## Common pitfalls

- **No operational team is listed**: ask your Division Lead or administrator to attach you to your Team under My Organization.
- **Want to change My Assistant's business hat?** Open "Account Settings › Personalization" to pick your default role lens.
- **Need to renew a temporary password?** Click the account edit action to jump directly to Account & Security.
